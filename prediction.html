Ganga River Water Quality Prediction

The AI system uses historical water quality dataset and Random Forest algorithm to forecast river health conditions.

AI Prediction Process

1. Dataset Selection

The system selects water quality records from the dataset.

2. AI Model Processing

Random Forest algorithm analyzes water parameters.

3. Prediction Result

The system predicts river water quality status.

Parameters Used for Prediction

pH

Measures acidity and alkalinity of water.

Temperature

Monitors water temperature variations.

Dissolved Oxygen

Indicates oxygen availability for aquatic life.

Turbidity

Measures water clarity and suspended particles.

BOD

Shows organic pollution level in water.
